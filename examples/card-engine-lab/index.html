Selection and drag help

Click to select, Ctrl/Cmd-click to toggle, or Shift-click for a range within a zone. Touch selection enables tap-to-toggle; enable Touch drag to carry the selection.

Focus a card—click its face or select it in the Cards box—then press S to toggle its selection. Shift+Arrow extends a range, Ctrl/Cmd-A selects in scope, and Escape clears. Space picks up the selection. While carrying, Arrow keys choose a slot, Tab changes zones, Enter or Space drops, and Escape cancels.

Set up batch places cards in Lake and River. Drag the selection to Ocean, Reject, then drag again and Accept. Flip selection once leaves the cards in place.
