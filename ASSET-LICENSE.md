# Asset licensing

The Apache-2.0 license in `LICENSE` applies to Cardinal software and its
documentation. It does not automatically apply to visual assets.

The artwork and logos in the repository, including the files in
`examples/card-engine-lab/` and the root-level logo files, are separate assets.
Their reuse and redistribution rights must be confirmed independently before
using them outside this repository or the public lab demonstration. See the
background-preset notes in
[`examples/card-engine-lab/assets/backgrounds/README.md`](examples/card-engine-lab/assets/backgrounds/README.md).
