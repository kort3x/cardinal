# Outcome

Inspect a simplified card in a readable view, browse related permitted content, and switch its active face independently of concealment.

## Design and constraints

Build fresh code in `packages/card-engine/` and new examples, not incremental changes to legacy engine files. Backward compatibility is out of scope. Read the full accepted requirements in parent specification #1; this slice adds only the behavior listed below. Keep app/lab labels and accessibility text German; repository docs/comments/tests English. Use the same public interface in consumers and tests. Do not publish/deploy as part of implementation.

## Scope

- Implement named content faces, a separate concealed back/sleeve, and coordinated flip ownership. Changing active content face while concealed does not reveal it.
- Provide in-place inspection and separate read-only preview sessions with viewport clamping, long-content scrolling, related content, live updates and clean dismissal.
- Compose hover/focus/selected/actionable/disabled/pending feedback; prevent nested controls from triggering parent actions. Provide dwell, keyboard and touch behavior.
- Inspection uses full permitted presentation over zone simplification. Preserve one scene shell and separately identified read-only auxiliary views with no duplicate gameplay controls.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Preview content updates live and closes on removal; focus is restored validly and previews do not steal focus unexpectedly.
- [ ] Unknown/concealed data is absent from unauthorized previews, accessibility and asset requests.
- [ ] Switch content faces, reveal/conceal and inspect during movement without stale restoration, replacement of the scene shell, or conflicting flip drivers.
- [ ] Touch, keyboard and reduced motion remain usable with cards near screen edges.

## Runnable lab scenario

Inspect a distant image-only card, switch between two content faces, conceal/reveal it, and update its content while the preview is open.
